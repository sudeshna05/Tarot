Reference Guide

Tarot Card Meanings

Upright and reversed meanings for all 78 cards — Major Arcana and the four suits of the Minor Arcana. Use this as a reference before, during, or after a reading.

Major Arcana

The 22 Major Arcana cards represent archetypal forces and significant life themes. When they appear in a reading, they often carry more weight than the Minor Arcana — pointing to deeper patterns, turning points, or forces larger than day-to-day circumstance. See also: Major Arcana guide.

0 · The Fool

↑ Beginnings · freedom · innocence

↓ Recklessness · naivety · holding back

Upright: A moment of pure possibility — stepping into the unknown with trust. Reversed: Enthusiasm without grounding, or fear preventing the first step.

I · The Magician

↑ Willpower · skill · resourcefulness

↓ Manipulation · poor planning · untapped talent

Upright: You have the tools. The question is whether you are using them. Reversed: Talent lying dormant, or ability directed toward self-deception.

II · The High Priestess

↑ Intuition · mystery · inner knowing

↓ Hidden agendas · disconnection · ignoring instincts

Upright: Something is known beneath the surface — trust what you already sense. Reversed: Disconnection from inner guidance, or secrets withheld.

III · The Empress

↑ Abundance · nurturing · creativity

↓ Dependence · creative block · neglect

Upright: Fertile ground — creative energy, generosity, and natural abundance. Reversed: A blocked creative flow, or over-reliance on others for nourishment.

IV · The Emperor

↑ Structure · authority · stability

↓ Rigidity · control · lack of discipline

Upright: Order, discipline, and a firm foundation. Reversed: Control becoming inflexibility, or structure collapsing through neglect.

V · The Hierophant

↑ Tradition · institutions · guidance

↓ Rebellion · unorthodoxy · questioning convention

Upright: Established wisdom, institutions, and the comfort of received knowledge. Reversed: A pull away from convention, or the need to find your own path.

VI · The Lovers

↑ Love · harmony · choices

↓ Disharmony · imbalance · misalignment

Upright: Union, meaningful choice, and alignment with what you truly value. Reversed: Dissonance between what you want and what you are choosing.

VII · The Chariot

↑ Determination · control · victory

↓ Lack of direction · aggression · opposition

Upright: Forward movement through focused will — conflicting forces harnessed. Reversed: Momentum without direction, or resistance winning for now.

VIII · Strength

↑ Courage · patience · inner strength

↓ Self-doubt · weakness · insecurity

Upright: Quiet power — not force, but the calm certainty that moves through fear. Reversed: Self-doubt eroding what inner strength you already hold.

IX · The Hermit

↑ Solitude · reflection · inner guidance

↓ Isolation · withdrawal · loneliness

Upright: A necessary withdrawal to find answers within. Reversed: Isolation that has become avoidance rather than wisdom.

X · Wheel of Fortune

↑ Cycles · fate · turning points

↓ Resistance to change · bad luck · setback

Upright: The turn of a cycle — fortune shifting, as it always does. Reversed: Resisting the inevitable movement of things, or a temporary downturn.

XI · Justice

↑ Fairness · truth · cause and effect

↓ Dishonesty · injustice · avoidance

Upright: Things being weighed clearly — truth, consequence, and accountability. Reversed: A situation where fairness is obscured or avoided.

XII · The Hanged Man

↑ Pause · surrender · new perspective

↓ Stagnation · avoidance · delays

Upright: A voluntary suspension — seeing the situation from a completely different angle. Reversed: Delay that is not purposeful, or avoidance dressed as reflection.

XIII · Death

↑ Endings · transformation · transition

↓ Resistance to change · stagnation · fear

Upright: Something is ending to make way for something new — rarely literal, always significant. Reversed: Clinging to what no longer serves, or fear of the necessary transition.

XIV · Temperance

↑ Balance · patience · moderation

↓ Excess · imbalance · lack of perspective

Upright: The art of integration — finding the middle path through patience and flow. Reversed: Extremes pulling against each other, or perspective lost to urgency.

XV · The Devil

↑ Bondage · materialism · shadow self

↓ Liberation · releasing chains · awareness

Upright: A pattern that has become a trap — often one you are participating in. Reversed: The recognition that you can step free — the chains were never locked.

XVI · The Tower

↑ Sudden change · upheaval · revelation

↓ Avoidance of disaster · fear of change

Upright: A sudden rupture that clears what could not be sustained — disruptive but clarifying. Reversed: Avoiding necessary disruption, or the fear of it looming.

XVII · The Star

↑ Hope · renewal · inspiration

↓ Despair · discouragement · disconnection

Upright: After difficulty, a quiet return of hope — not certainty, but direction. Reversed: A dimming of that light, or the struggle to believe renewal is possible.

XVIII · The Moon

↑ Illusion · fear · the subconscious

↓ Confusion lifting · truth emerging

Upright: Things are not as clear as they seem — fear or illusion may be distorting the picture. Reversed: The fog beginning to lift, reality becoming more legible.

XIX · The Sun

↑ Joy · success · vitality

↓ Temporary setback · overshadowing · delayed joy

Upright: Clarity, warmth, and genuine success — one of the most positive cards in the deck. Reversed: Joy temporarily obscured, or success delayed but not denied.

XX · Judgement

↑ Reflection · reckoning · awakening

↓ Self-doubt · refusal to learn · avoidance

Upright: A moment of honest reckoning — looking clearly at where you are and what is being asked of you. Reversed: Avoiding the call to account, or harsh self-judgment.

XXI · The World

↑ Completion · integration · achievement

↓ Incompletion · loose ends · seeking closure

Upright: A cycle fully completed — wholeness, integration, and the satisfaction of having arrived. Reversed: Something still unresolved, a chapter not yet properly closed.

Minor Arcana

The 56 Minor Arcana cards reflect the texture of daily life — practical challenges, emotional states, thoughts, and material concerns. They are organized into four suits, each associated with a classical element. See also: Minor Arcana guide.

⁂

Wands · Fire

Wands carry the energy of fire: passion, creativity, ambition, and driven action. They speak to will, vision, and the spark that initiates things.

Ace of Wands

↑ Inspiration · new beginnings · creative spark

Upright: A burst of creative energy — the seed of something new. Reversed: Delayed start, or creative energy blocked before it can ignite.

Two of Wands

↑ Planning · future vision · bold decisions

Upright: Standing at a threshold, surveying what lies ahead and deciding. Reversed: Fear of moving beyond the familiar, or poorly conceived plans.

Three of Wands

↑ Expansion · foresight · opportunity

Upright: What you set in motion is beginning to return — expansion is underway. Reversed: Obstacles slowing forward movement, or opportunities passing unrecognized.

Four of Wands

↑ Celebration · harmony · home

Upright: A moment of genuine achievement worth marking — stability and shared joy. Reversed: Instability in a place that should feel safe, or delayed celebration.

Five of Wands

↑ Conflict · competition · disagreement

Upright: Energy scattered in all directions — competing voices, friction, or healthy challenge. Reversed: Avoiding necessary conflict, or conflict finally resolving.

Six of Wands

↑ Victory · recognition · progress

Upright: Success acknowledged — effort bearing visible fruit. Reversed: Delayed recognition, or ego inflated by early success.

Seven of Wands

↑ Perseverance · defense · standing firm

Upright: Holding your ground despite opposition — you are further ahead than it feels. Reversed: Exhaustion from constant defense, or capitulating under pressure.

Eight of Wands

↑ Swift movement · speed · communication

Upright: Things moving fast now — messages arriving, momentum building. Reversed: Delays, miscommunication, or energy dispersed before it lands.

Nine of Wands

↑ Resilience · persistence · final stretch

Upright: Worn but unbroken — you are closer to the end than you think. Reversed: Exhaustion finally winning, or past wounds making you over-cautious.

Ten of Wands

↑ Burden · responsibility · hard work

Upright: Carrying too much — success has multiplied obligation. Reversed: Learning to put some of it down, or collapsing under the weight.

Page of Wands

↑ Enthusiasm · exploration · new ideas

Upright: Creative curiosity — a new direction calling for your attention. Reversed: Scattered ideas without follow-through, or reckless enthusiasm.

Knight of Wands

↑ Action · adventure · energy

Upright: Bold, fast-moving energy — charging toward what excites you. Reversed: Haste creating problems, or restlessness that can't settle.

Queen of Wands

↑ Confidence · determination · charisma

Upright: Someone who knows their own power — warm, magnetic, and purposeful. Reversed: Self-doubt beneath the surface, or charisma turned toward jealousy.

King of Wands

↑ Leadership · vision · entrepreneurship

Upright: Visionary leadership — the ability to inspire and build. Reversed: Impulsiveness overriding good judgment, or leadership that becomes domineering.

Cups · Water

Cups carry the energy of water: emotion, intuition, relationships, and the inner life. They speak to feeling, imagination, and all that flows beneath the surface.

Ace of Cups

↑ Love · new feelings · emotional beginnings

Upright: An opening of the heart — new feeling, new connection, creative flow. Reversed: Emotional energy blocked, or a connection that hasn't yet been allowed.

Two of Cups

↑ Partnership · connection · mutual attraction

Upright: A meaningful meeting of two — mutual recognition and harmony. Reversed: A partnership out of balance, or attraction without genuine reciprocity.

Three of Cups

↑ Celebration · friendship · community

Upright: Shared joy and the warmth of true connection. Reversed: Overindulgence, exclusion, or friendship becoming competitive.

Four of Cups

↑ Contemplation · apathy · missed opportunity

Upright: Looking inward while the world extends an offering — something may be passing unnoticed. Reversed: Emerging from contemplation, or motivation returning after a quiet period.

Five of Cups

↑ Loss · grief · regret

Upright: Mourning what has been lost — real and valid, but two cups still stand behind you. Reversed: Beginning to turn toward what remains, acceptance finding its way in.

Six of Cups

↑ Nostalgia · innocence · childhood

Upright: A return to simpler feelings — memory, kindness, and what once brought joy. Reversed: Living too fully in the past, or nostalgia preventing movement.

Seven of Cups

↑ Fantasy · choices · illusion

Upright: Multiple possibilities, not all of which are real — discernment required. Reversed: Cutting through the fog to see which path is actually viable.

Eight of Cups

↑ Walking away · seeking meaning · transition

Upright: Leaving behind something that no longer fulfils — a search for deeper meaning. Reversed: Unable to leave despite knowing it's time, or returning to what was left.

Nine of Cups

↑ Contentment · wishes · satisfaction

Upright: The wish card — a deep sense that things are, for now, enough. Reversed: Satisfaction built on material comfort alone, or a wish unfulfilled.

Ten of Cups

↑ Harmony · family · fulfillment

Upright: Emotional completion — lasting joy, connection, and belonging. Reversed: Disconnection within what should be harmonious, or an ideal at odds with reality.

Page of Cups

↑ Sensitivity · creativity · intuitive messages

Upright: A tender, imaginative energy — pay attention to what is emerging from the depths. Reversed: Emotional immaturity, or creative sensitivity turned toward drama.

Knight of Cups

↑ Romance · following the heart · idealism

Upright: Leading with feeling — a romantic, idealistic energy in pursuit of what moves you. Reversed: Moodiness, jealousy, or idealism that avoids practical reality.

Queen of Cups

↑ Compassion · intuition · emotional security

Upright: Deep empathy and a calm, secure emotional presence. Reversed: Emotional instability, or compassion that has become codependency.

King of Cups

↑ Emotional balance · wisdom · diplomacy

Upright: Mastery of the emotional realm — wise, balanced, and dependable in feeling. Reversed: Emotional manipulation, or feelings suppressed beneath a calm exterior.

Swords · Air

Swords carry the energy of air: thought, communication, conflict, and the search for truth. They speak to the mind, decisions, and the clarity — or confusion — that thinking brings.

Ace of Swords

↑ Clarity · breakthrough · truth

Upright: A cut through confusion — sudden clarity, a decision made, truth spoken. Reversed: Confusion, a truth being withheld, or clarity that wounds unnecessarily.

Two of Swords

↑ Indecision · stalemate · blocked emotions

Upright: A standoff — refusing to look at something, or caught between two paths. Reversed: The blindfold coming off, information arriving to help you decide.

Three of Swords

↑ Heartbreak · grief · sorrow

Upright: Pain that is real — loss, betrayal, or sadness that must be felt before it can pass. Reversed: Beginning to heal, forgiveness opening a way through.

Four of Swords

↑ Rest · recovery · contemplation

Upright: A necessary pause — retreat to restore before returning to the world. Reversed: Restlessness that prevents recovery, or a return before full readiness.

Five of Swords

↑ Conflict · defeat · betrayal

Upright: A battle that has left damage behind — even winners sometimes pay too high a price. Reversed: Reconciliation becoming possible, or choosing peace over being right.

Six of Swords

↑ Transition · moving on · calmer waters

Upright: Moving away from turbulence — not yet at the destination, but finally moving. Reversed: Emotional baggage making the crossing harder, or resisting necessary departure.

Seven of Swords

↑ Strategy · deception · cunning

Upright: Something being taken without permission — deception, or strategic retreat. Reversed: Coming clean about something, or deception being uncovered.

Eight of Swords

↑ Restriction · feeling trapped · self-limitation

Upright: Feeling bound — often by one's own perception more than external circumstance. Reversed: Recognizing the binds are not as fixed as they seemed; a way out emerging.

Nine of Swords

↑ Anxiety · nightmares · despair

Upright: The 3 a.m. mind — fears amplified by darkness and isolation. Reversed: A turn toward hope, or beginning to seek help with what has been carried alone.

Ten of Swords

↑ Painful endings · crisis · betrayal

Upright: A definitive ending — things cannot continue as they were. Reversed: Gradual healing, or resisting an ending that has already occurred.

Page of Swords

↑ Curiosity · vigilance · communication

Upright: Sharp, watchful intelligence — gathering information before acting. Reversed: Gossip, jumping to conclusions, or words without wisdom behind them.

Knight of Swords

↑ Ambition · haste · direct action

Upright: Fast, decisive, unstoppable — cutting through to the point. Reversed: Recklessness without consideration for what is cut down in the rush.

Queen of Swords

↑ Clarity · perceptiveness · independence

Upright: Sharp discernment and hard-won independence — clear-eyed without cruelty. Reversed: Bitterness shaping judgment, or clarity used as a weapon.

King of Swords

↑ Intellect · authority · clear thinking

Upright: Mastery of thought — fair, principled, and deeply analytical. Reversed: Intellect turned to manipulation, or authority wielded without compassion.

Pentacles · Earth

Pentacles carry the energy of earth: the material world, resources, work, the body, and the practical. They speak to what is built, tended, and made tangible over time.

Ace of Pentacles

↑ Opportunity · prosperity · new resources

Upright: A concrete opportunity — the seed of material stability or a new practical beginning. Reversed: Lost opportunity, or resources arriving before the groundwork is ready.

Two of Pentacles

↑ Balance · adaptability · juggling priorities

Upright: Managing multiple demands with grace — adaptable and in motion. Reversed: Balance tipping, overwhelm breaking the rhythm.

Three of Pentacles

↑ Teamwork · mastery · skill building

Upright: Craft being recognized and refined through collaboration. Reversed: Work done in isolation when collaboration was needed, or mediocrity accepted.

Four of Pentacles

↑ Security · conservatism · holding on

Upright: Guarding what you have built — stability, but also the risk of scarcity thinking. Reversed: Loosening the grip, or hoarding giving way to generosity.

Five of Pentacles

↑ Hardship · poverty · isolation

Upright: A difficult stretch — financial or spiritual poverty, feeling left out in the cold. Reversed: Help found, a corner turned, or the light of the window finally noticed.

Six of Pentacles

↑ Generosity · sharing · giving and receiving

Upright: The flow of resources — generosity extended, or receiving what you need. Reversed: Charity with strings attached, or imbalance in the giving dynamic.

Seven of Pentacles

↑ Patience · long-term vision · investment

Upright: Pausing to assess what your effort is producing — the long game. Reversed: Impatience undermining a slow-growing investment, or effort without adequate return.

Eight of Pentacles

↑ Diligence · mastery · skill development

Upright: The work of becoming — focused, repetitive effort toward mastery. Reversed: Going through the motions without real engagement, or lacking discipline.

Nine of Pentacles

↑ Luxury · self-sufficiency · abundance

Upright: The rewards of sustained effort — independence, refinement, abundance cultivated alone. Reversed: Material comfort masking deeper lack, or financial dependence.

Ten of Pentacles

↑ Wealth · legacy · stability

Upright: Lasting wealth and the satisfaction of what has been built across time. Reversed: Financial instability, or the cost of prioritizing legacy over living.

Page of Pentacles

↑ Ambition · learning · practicality

Upright: Early-stage ambition with a realistic, grounded quality. Reversed: Lack of follow-through, or ambition without the willingness to do the work.

Knight of Pentacles

↑ Hard work · reliability · thoroughness

Upright: Steady, methodical, and trustworthy — slow but certain progress. Reversed: Stubbornness, or effort that has stagnated into routine without growth.

Queen of Pentacles

↑ Nurturing · practicality · financial security

Upright: Warmth expressed through practical care — creating a stable, nourishing environment. Reversed: Self-neglect in service of others, or security undermined by imbalance.

King of Pentacles

↑ Wealth · leadership · reliability

Upright: Mastery of the material world — a steady, generous, and accomplished presence. Reversed: Materialism overshadowing deeper values, or financial authority misused.
